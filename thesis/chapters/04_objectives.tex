\chapter{Objectives}\label{cap:objectives}

\section{General objective}

To analyze the impacts of adult neurogenesis on pattern separation and population sparsity in the DG-CA3 circuit in a
computational model based on experimental data.

\section{Specific objectives}

\begin{itemize}
    \item To analyze the effects of adult neurogenesis on DG pattern separation under different conditions of input pattern
    similarity.
    \item To consider the effects of incomplete iGC circuit integration, simulating different EC$\to$iGC connection
    probabilities.
    \item To analyze the effects of iGC circuit integration on CA3 pattern separation and population sparsity, and to determine
    whether the changes in separation follow from sparser coding or from the decorrelation of the outputs.
    \item To reproduce \textit{in silico} the direct activation of the iGCs performed \textit{in vivo}, characterizing the
    response it evokes in each population of the DG and CA3.
\end{itemize}
